# Atlas Brand Spec (Phase 0)

## Identity

- **Name**: Atlas. Never "DripAdvisor", never "Muse", never provider names in
  user-facing surfaces (provider names appear only in source health /
  diagnostics views).
- **Concept**: "the quiet map of your next move" — orientation, evidence,
  calm momentum.

## Logo

- **Wordmark**: "Atlas" set in Space Grotesk (SemiBold), letter-spacing +2%,
  ink-on-paper or paper-on-graphite.
- **Mark**: compact route/compass mark — three waypoints connected by a route
  line with a soft gap before the destination node (uncertainty is part of
  the brand). Current status: **internal placeholder** drawn from the design
  system; a final team-approved mark remains an **asset dependency**.
- Variants: light (ink on paper), dark (paper on graphite), monochrome.
- Minimum sizes: wordmark 96px wide; mark 24px (favicon/PWA maskable variants
  at 192px and 512px).

## Typography

- **Space Grotesk** — display/editorial statements only.
- **DM Sans** — body, UI, data labels.
- Tabular numerals for amounts, counts, dates, confidence.

## Color tokens

See `docs/atlas-design-system.md` §1 for the authoritative table:
warm paper / graphite / lime (ready) / lilac (reflective) / amber
(review-needed) / red (blocked or destructive only) / low-contrast lines.

## Icon rules

- Single stroke weight, rounded joins, 20px grid, 1.5px stroke at 20px.
- Icon-only controls always carry accessible labels.
- No filled emoji-style icons in product chrome.

## Image usage rules

- Garment imagery appears only in wardrobe/looks contexts.
- Generated previews always display: "AI-generated visual approximation;
  garment details and fit may not be exact."
- No person imagery in shared marketing surfaces without separate consent;
  try-on outputs are never used in promotional material.
